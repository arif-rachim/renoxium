# Image credits

All images on the public site pages are screenshots of Renoxium demo templates, taken from the
demos the server provisions at startup (`demo-<example>` apps) with Chromium at a 390x844 phone
viewport (device scale 2) and stored as WebP, 600 pixels wide. The demo banner is hidden in the
screenshots and, where it covered the content, the floating WhatsApp button too. Everything shown
is demo data: the product, service, tour and listing photos are the demos' own photos (sources and
licences in `examples/demo-images/CREDITS.md`), and the bookings are the schedule every demo gets
around the current day (`internal/platform/demodata.go`).
