# Image credits

All images on the public site pages are screenshots of Renoxium demo templates, taken from the
demos the server provisions at startup (`demo-<example>` apps) with Chromium at a 390x844 phone
viewport (device scale 2) and stored as WebP, 600 pixels wide. The demo banner is hidden in the
screenshots. Everything shown is demo data: the banner, product and service photos are the demos'
own photos (sources and licences in `examples/demo-images/CREDITS.md`), and the bookings are the
schedule every demo gets around the current day (`internal/platform/demodata.go`).
