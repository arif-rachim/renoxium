Add Renoxium to ChatGPT once; the free plan is enough. In ChatGPT open Plugins, choose Add, then Add custom MCP
server. Name it Renoxium, enter https://www.renoxium.com/mcp, keep OAuth, tick "I understand" and choose Create as a
plugin. Then sign in to Renoxium and choose Allow. In a chat, choose Renoxium once from the + menu (or type
@Renoxium) and ask "What apps do I have?". After that, ChatGPT uses Renoxium by itself when you ask about your apps.
When Renoxium adds new abilities, choose Refresh tools in the plugin's settings.
