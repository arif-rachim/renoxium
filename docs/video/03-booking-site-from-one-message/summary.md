in your business name and checks every page, including on a phone. ChatGPT asks before anything is created. The site
appears on a private preview that only you can see, and nothing goes live until you publish it. The services, prices and
photos start as samples; the next lesson changes them to yours. There are no credits, so you can ask for changes as often
as you like.
