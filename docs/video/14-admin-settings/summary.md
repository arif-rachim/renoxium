The settings pages are separate entries in the sidebar:
- Notifications: where booking and order alerts go, by email or WhatsApp.
- Staff: invite helpers. They can work in the admin panel but not publish, change the plan or delete the app.
- Domain: your own web address.
- Connections: allow the outside services your site may call, and test calls from the preview.
- Rename app.
- Delete app: download an export of all your data there first.

Open preview takes you to your private preview at any time.
