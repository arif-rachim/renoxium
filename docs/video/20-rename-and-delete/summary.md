To rename or delete an app, just ask. ChatGPT can only prepare it: you get a link and confirm on renoxium.com with your
password. After a rename, the old address sends visitors to the new one for a year, and you can rename once every 30
days. Before deleting, download an export. Then type the app's name and your password to delete it for good. Nothing
changes until you confirm.

need it."
