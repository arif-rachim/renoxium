# Horizontal competitors and adoption evidence for AI/no-code builders among UAE/GCC small businesses (2025-2026)

Research date: 18 Nov 2026. Conversions use the pegs USD 1 = AED 3.6725 and SAR 1 = AED 0.979; AED figures marked "≈" are my conversions, not vendor list prices. "Search summary" means the figure came from a search-result digest and the underlying page was not opened; treat as lower confidence. Pages actually opened are marked "(fetched)".

## 1. Horizontal builders, storefront tools and payment-link apps: prices and UAE localisation

### Takeaway
A UAE small business can get a hosted store for roughly AED 0-120/month (Zid/Salla free or SAR 99; Shopify Basic USD 32 ≈ AED 118; Take.app free-USD 19; Mamo/Ziina payment links at AED 0/month plus ~2.6-2.9% + AED 1), so Renoxium's AED 99 tier sits exactly at the regional "entry paid plan" price point (Zid Rise and Salla Plus are both SAR 99). None of the international builders publish AED subscription prices; Shopify bills in USD even on its UAE page, and Zid/Salla are Saudi-centric (about 1.3% of Zid stores are in the UAE).

### Inferences
- Renoxium's Free + 2% card fee mirrors Shopify's 2% third-party-gateway surcharge on Basic and undercuts Zbooni's historical 3.5%; on top of Stripe's 2.9% + AED 1 the all-in card cost on Renoxium Free is about 4.9% + AED 1, higher than Mamo/Ziina links (2.6-2.9% + AED 1 with no platform fee). Payment-link apps are the cheapest "good enough" substitute for businesses that only need to get paid.
- AED 99/month equals Mamo Premium (AED 99) and is within 2% of Zid Rise / Salla Plus (SAR 99), and is below Shopify Basic (≈ AED 118 + gateway surcharge). AED 299 matches Zid Growth / Salla Pro (SAR 299) and is below Shopify Grow (≈ AED 338).
- Localisation is where the horizontals are weakest for UAE: Shopify/Wix/Squarespace bill in USD and treat Arabic/RTL as theme or add-on work; Zid/Salla are Arabic-native but built around Saudi rules (ZATCA, Saudi gateways) with a tiny UAE footprint. Native AED + 5% VAT + COD + bilingual RTL out of the box is a real differentiator, but only against store builders, not against vertical SaaS.
- Store builders cover catalog commerce well; the gap that horizontal tools leave is non-store workflows (bookings with deposits, quotes, job tracking, memberships, custom admin panels), which is where a "custom app from chat" product is least substitutable.

### Gaps
- Official AED subscription prices for Wix, Squarespace, Hostinger, Bitrix24 and Odoo were not found (vendors bill USD or geo-price at checkout).
- Zoho Commerce, Zoho Bookings and Zoho Creator AED prices were not retrieved; only Zoho Books/One/CRM figures surfaced.
- Zbooni's current (2026) plan prices and cLink pricing were not found; only 2019-2021 fee reporting.
- Linktree pricing and UAE usage not researched (ran out of budget).
- Shopify native COD and Arabic theme support in the UAE not confirmed from official docs; Shopify Payments UAE status is contradictory across sources.

## 2. AI app builders: pricing, credit models, complaints, GCC/Arabic-first entrants

### Takeaway
Mainstream AI builders cluster at USD 20-25/month (≈ AED 73-92) with credit/token metering, and the dominant complaint from non-technical users is unpredictable credit burn (fixes cost the same credits as features) plus backend lock-in. Arabic-first AI builders now exist (Myndlab from Dubai, Fekra AI, Mawqei, Hallix, bilarabi.ai) but all are 2026 launches with only press-release evidence.

### Cited Findings
- Lovable (fetched): Free = 5 build credits/day up to 30/month; Pro $25/month (≈ AED 92) with 100 credits; Business $50/month (≈ AED 184); custom domains on paid plans only; credits non-refundable; rollover rules stated inconsistently on its own page — [Lovable pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing)
- Lovable complaints cluster on opaque credit usage; small fixes draw from the same pool as new features — [Banani: Lovable pricing and hidden costs 2026](https://www.banani.co/blog/lovable-pricing-and-credits); [Coddy Lovable review](https://coddy.tech/build/vs/lovable)
- Bolt.new: free tier ~300K tokens/day, Pro $25/month (token allowance differs by source); costs spike late in a build as projects grow — [Builder Price Watch](https://koenmatt.github.io/builderpricewatch/); [Rework: Lovable alternatives 2026](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-coding/best-lovable-alternatives)
- v0 (Vercel): free ~$5 credits/month; Premium $20/month including $20 credits — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-coding/best-lovable-alternatives)
- Replit: effort-based agent pricing; users report bills of hundreds of dollars per month in heavy agent sessions; Core about $240/year — [creativeainews AI app builder pricing 2026](https://www.creativeainews.com/articles/ai-app-builder-pricing-2026/); [Omid Saffari comparison](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/lovable-alternatives)
- Base44 (Wix-owned): free up to 5 message credits/day (25/month); Starter about $240/year; dual credit system where runtime "integration credits" grow with app usage; exports frontend only, backend bound to its SDK — [Omid Saffari comparison](https://omidsaffari.com/blog/lovable-alternatives); [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/tools/ai-coding/best-lovable-alternatives)
- Hostinger Horizons (reported as rebranded "Hostinger AI Builder" by mid-2026): 7-day trial then from $6.99/month (≈ AED 26) — [Website Builder Expert](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/vibe-coding/hostinger-horizons-pricing/)
- Wix "Harmony" AI is included on every Wix plan including free — [aitoolduels comparison](https://aitoolduels.pages.dev/ai-website-builder-tools-wix-vs-hostinger-vs-10web)
- Durable: about $22-25/month (sources vary, one says $12 annual); 10Web: AI Starter $10/month intro, doubling at renewal — [web3aiblog: real price after intro offer](https://www.web3aiblog.com/blog/best-ai-website-builders-2026); [G2 10Web pricing](https://www.g2.com/products/10web/pricing)
- Myndlab (Permus Software House, Dubai): "region's first native Arabic AI application builder", beta June 2026, global launch 6 Oct 2026 at Ai Everything Abu Dhabi; US became its largest user market — [Zawya press release](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/uaes-permus-software-house-launches-myndlab-the-regions-first-native-arabic-ai-application-builder-qmbq6qhn); [Zawya follow-up](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/dubai-built-myndlab-goes-global-following-beta-with-us-emerging-as-its-largest-user-market-1533128)
- Other Arabic/RTL-first builders seen on Product Hunt in 2026: bilarabi.ai (RTL landing pages, Feb 2026), Mawqei, Fekra AI; Hallix (UAE AI website builder) — [bilarabi.ai launch](https://www.producthunt.com/posts/bilarabi-ai); [Mawqei](https://www.producthunt.com/p/mawqei/why-arabic-support-matters-here)

### Inferences
- Renoxium's flat AED 99/299 with no build credits (the user's own ChatGPT/Claude subscription pays for generation) directly answers the top complaint about Lovable/Bolt/Replit; but the total cost for an owner without a paid AI plan is AED 99 + ChatGPT Plus (≈ AED 75-80) or Claude Pro.
- None of the mainstream AI builders ship UAE primitives (AED/VAT invoices, COD, WhatsApp login); users must prompt these into existence and maintain them, which favours a platform with them built in.
- Myndlab's largest market being the US suggests Arabic-first positioning alone has not yet produced strong GCC SME pull (weak evidence, single press release).

### Gaps
- No independent survey of non-technical users' complaints; evidence is blogs and review aggregators, many vendor-affiliated.
- Shopify Sidekick/Magic pricing and limits not researched (bundled with Shopify plans as far as known, unverified here).
- No pricing or user numbers for the Arabic-first builders.

## 3. ChatGPT and Claude custom MCP connectors: plan availability, UAE access, ChatGPT commerce

### Takeaway
Claude is the low-friction path: Anthropic's help centre states custom remote-MCP connectors work on Free (one connector), Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise, across web, desktop and mobile, and the UAE is a supported country. ChatGPT is uncertain: developer mode exists for Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise/Edu on the web (not Free/Go), but OpenAI's own documents are reported to conflict on whether Plus/Pro get write actions ("full MCP"), which Renoxium's build flow requires. ChatGPT Instant Checkout is US-only as far as any source shows.

### Cited Findings
- Claude (fetched, official): custom connectors using remote MCP are "available on Claude, Cowork, and Claude Desktop for users on Free, Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, and Enterprise plans"; "Free users are limited to one custom connector"; on Team only Owners/Primary Owners add connectors, members then connect individually; setup applies "across every Claude client, including claude.ai, Claude Desktop, Cowork, and the mobile apps" — [Anthropic help: custom connectors using remote MCP](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11175166-get-started-with-custom-connectors-using-remote-mcp.md)
- Claude.ai lists the United Arab Emirates as a supported country — [Anthropic supported countries](https://anthropic.com/supported-countries)
- ChatGPT: OpenAI announced developer mode with full MCP tool support (write actions, not just search/fetch) for Plus and Pro in Sept 2025 — [OpenAI Developers on X](https://x.com/OpenAIDevs/status/1965807401745207708)
- ChatGPT conflict (docs checked by a third party on 21 Oct 2026): OpenAI developer docs say developer mode is "Available to Pro, Plus, Business, Enterprise, and Education accounts on the web", while the help centre says "Full MCP is only available to Business and Enterprise/Edu users, currently"; the guide recommends testing a write action before relying on Plus/Pro — [Tokenrip: add MCP to ChatGPT (fetched)](https://tokenrip.com/blog/add-mcp-to-chatgpt)
- Boomi's documentation, citing OpenAI's help centre, says Plus and Pro are limited to read/fetch-only custom connectors, with full write support on Business/Enterprise/Edu; on Business a workspace admin must enable developer mode — [Boomi: Connect ChatGPT setup](https://help.boomi.com/docs/Atomsphere/Platform/Connect_chatgpt_setup)
- Free and Go plans cannot add custom MCP connectors (secondary sources citing OpenAI's plan table) — [OpenAI help: developer mode and MCP connectors (403 when fetched; not read directly)](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461-developer-mode-and-mcp-connectors)
- OpenAI's Apps SDK deploy doc says only that "Account and workspace policies apply to adding and using custom MCP servers" and names no plans — [OpenAI Apps SDK: connect ChatGPT (fetched)](https://developers.openai.com/apps-sdk/deploy/connect-chatgpt)
- OpenAI lists the UAE among supported countries for ChatGPT and the API — [OpenAI supported countries](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7947663-chatgpt-and-openai-api-supported-countries-and-territories)
- Apps in ChatGPT (directory) launched Oct 2025 for logged-in Free/Go/Plus/Pro users outside the EU; developers choose country availability per app — [TestingCatalog](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-launches-chatgpt-apps-and-sdk-preview-outside-eu.md)
- UAE consumer prices (App Store listings, channel-specific): ChatGPT Go AED 29.99/month, Plus AED 79.99/month — [techyorker: AI subscription costs in the UAE](https://techyorker.com/how-much-do-ai-subscriptions-cost-in-the-uae/)
- Instant Checkout launched Sept 2025 for US users with Etsy (Shopify "coming"), on the open Agentic Commerce Protocol co-developed with Stripe; as of Feb 2026 it supported US-based transactions and single-item purchases only — [Digital Commerce 360, Feb 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/02/16/openai-expands-agentic-commerce-push/); [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-debuts-new-chatgpt-buy-button-and-open-source-agentic-commerce)
- In March 2026 Shopify's ChatGPT integration plan changed to "ChatGPT in Agentic Storefronts" rather than the originally planned Instant Checkout integration — [Digital Commerce 360, Mar 2026](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/17/shopify-integration-with-chatgpt-changes/)
- Shopify's UAE pricing page now lists an "Agentic" plan at $0/month — [Shopify UAE pricing](https://www.shopify.com/ae/pricing)

### Inferences
- For a UAE micro-business owner, the cheapest working route to Renoxium is Claude Free (one custom connector, which Renoxium would occupy) or Claude Pro; ChatGPT requires at least Plus (≈ AED 80/month) and may not permit the write tools Renoxium needs unless on Business. This is a material funnel risk because ChatGPT is by far the more used assistant in the UAE (see section 4). It must be verified hands-on before the report states ChatGPT Plus works.
- No source shows ChatGPT in-chat checkout available to UAE merchants or buyers; Renoxium's per-app public MCP is therefore one of few ways a UAE small business can be queryable by AI assistants today, though customer-side demand for that is unproven.

### Gaps
- OpenAI's help-centre article could not be opened (HTTP 403), so the Plus/Pro write-action status in Nov 2026 is unresolved; sources conflict.
- No official statement on Instant Checkout / ACP availability in the UAE or GCC was found; absence of evidence only.
- Whether ChatGPT custom connectors work in the mobile apps for UAE users is not confirmed (docs say "on the web" for developer mode).

## 4. Assistant usage among UAE residents and SME owners; government AI initiatives

### Takeaway
The UAE has the highest measured generative-AI usage in the world (Microsoft: 64% of working-age population in H2 2025; a later reading of 73.3% in Q2 2026), and ChatGPT is the dominant tool, but paid business use is much lower: a transaction-based index found 29% of (Pemo-customer) SMEs paying for AI tools in Q3 2026. "Free ChatGPT Plus for all UAE residents" was a 2025 rumour that fact-checks rejected.

### Cited Findings
- Microsoft AI diffusion report: UAE ranked first of 147 countries with 64% of the working-age population using generative AI in H2 2025 — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/technology/uae-tops-global-rankings-in-ai-adoption-in-the-workplace-microsoft-report-1.500330512); [tbreak](https://tbreak.com/uae-leads-global-ai-adoption-2025/)
- A Nov 2026 press release citing Microsoft data says 73.3% of UAE people aged 15-64 used a generative AI tool in Q2 2026, and contrasts this with low paid adoption — [press release via ITBusinessNet](https://itbusinessnet.com/2026/11/the-uaes-ai-adoption-is-a-world-record-paying-for-it-is-another-story/)
- KPMG (Oct 2025): 97% of UAE survey respondents use AI for work, study or personal purposes — [KPMG UAE](https://kpmg.com/ae/en/media/press-releases/2025/10/97-percent-of-uae-residents-now-use-ai.html)
- An OpenAI executive said nearly 80% of UAE residents aged 18-34 use ChatGPT weekly (executive statement, not a published survey) — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/abu-dhabi/80-of-young-adults-use-chatgpt-weekly-openai-executive-says-1.500734485)
- LinkedIn poll: UAE professionals second-highest globally for AI-tool use — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/uae-professionals-second-highest-in-world-using-ai-tools-linkedin-poll-1.500246696)
- Businesses: UAE AI Office + AWS/Strand Partners survey of 1,000 firms reports 72% AI adoption, up from 53% a year earlier (self-reported by leaders) — [Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/research-studies/uae-ai-office-and-aws-announce-72-ai-adoption-rate-across-uae-businesses-1458623)
- Pemo SME Spend Index: 29% of businesses paid for AI tools in Q3 2026, led by technology and marketing firms; SME AI spending rose fivefold; based on Pemo card transactions, not a representative sample — [Enterprise IT World MEA](https://enterpriseitworldmea.com/uae-smes-increase-ai-spending-fivefold-as-adoption-spreads-across-industries/); [ITBusinessNet](https://itbusinessnet.com/2026/11/the-uaes-ai-adoption-is-a-world-record-paying-for-it-is-another-story/)

- Free ChatGPT Plus: May 2025 reports that all UAE residents would get Plus free were not confirmed by OpenAI or UAE authorities; Plus remained a paid plan — [Gulf News fact piece](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/is-openai-giving-chatgpt-for-free-to-all-uae-residents-know-whats-true-1.500141497); [Benzinga fact-check](https://benzinga.com/z/45612410); [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2025/05/27/chatgpt-plus-uae-openai-deal)
- Stargate UAE: 1 GW compute cluster in Abu Dhabi (G42, OpenAI, Oracle, Nvidia), first 200 MW expected in 2026; UAE described as first country to enable ChatGPT nationwide under "OpenAI for Countries" — [OpenAI: Introducing Stargate UAE](https://openai.com/index/introducing-stargate-uae)

### Inferences
- Awareness and casual use of ChatGPT are near-universal among younger UAE residents, so "build it by chatting with ChatGPT" needs little education; the bottleneck is paid subscriptions and the connector step, not familiarity.
- The 29% paid-AI figure (biased towards digitally mature Pemo customers) implies that most traditional SMEs (trades, salons, groceries, garages) do not yet hold a paid AI plan; tech, marketing and professional-services firms are the easiest early adopters.

### Gaps
- No ChatGPT vs Claude vs Gemini share figures for the UAE were found; Claude usage in the UAE specifically is undocumented.
- No representative survey of UAE SME owners' assistant usage; no official UAE government statistic on business AI use.
- No specific Dubai SME / Khalifa Fund AI programme details were found in this research.

## 5. Which verticals adopt vertical SaaS vs stay on WhatsApp/Excel; underserved verticals

### Takeaway
In the GCC, restaurants (Foodics: 33,500-40,000 branches) and online retail (Salla 68,000+, Zid ~13,500 merchants, mostly Saudi) are the verticals already captured by funded vertical/commerce SaaS; beauty has a strong global vendor (Fresha) now localised in Arabic; real estate has a dominant portal (Property Finder). Across UAE SMEs generally, online selling remains low (22% sell online) and Instagram/WhatsApp dominate, but I found no sector-by-sector GCC survey naming underserved verticals.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences

- Verticals for which this research surfaced no dominant GCC vendor (home services/maintenance, tutoring and training centres, small clinics and wellness practitioners, event/catering, tailoring, car workshops, cleaning, rental businesses) are plausibly the WhatsApp/Excel-run segments, but this is inference from the absence of merchant-count evidence, not from a survey; industry researchers should confirm per sector.

## 6. SME software budget, price sensitivity, freemium conversion and churn

### Takeaway
No reliable figure for the typical UAE SME monthly software budget exists in public sources. The best proxies are entry prices in the market (AED 0-120/month for stores, AED 60-135/month for Zoho) and the freemium benchmark that only about 2% of Wix's registered users pay.

### Cited Findings
- Wix FY2025: 304M+ registered users and 6.11M premium subscriptions, about 2% paying (ratio computed from company totals; historically under 3%) — [Colorlib Wix statistics](https://colorlib.com/wp/wix-statistics/); [Wix FY2025 results coverage](https://daytraders.com/news/2026/03/04/wix-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results)
- Generic freemium benchmark: roughly 3-5% free-to-paid for B2B SaaS; Weebly's first paid plan converted just under 0.5% (old) — vendor blogs — [Baremetrics](https://baremetrics.com/blog/freemium-saas-implode)
- Squarespace does not disclose churn; commentary says churn after price rises was "modest" — [SaaStr](https://www.saastr.com/5-interesting-learnings-from-squarespace-at-1-billion-in-arr)
- Horizontal SMB SaaS gross retention typically 78-85% per year (SaaS Capital via secondary) — [SaaS Mag](https://www.saasmag.com/vertical-saas-niche-beats-horizontal-2026/)
- GCC SaaS market estimated at USD 3.56bn in 2025, USD 8.61bn by 2032 (market-research estimate) — [MarkNtel Advisors](https://www.marknteladvisors.com/research-library/gcc-software-as-a-service-market-report.html)
- A platform vendor's report gives average monthly platform spend of USD 47 (pre-seed) and USD 189 (seed) for GCC startups — its own users, not representative — [Mewayz report](https://mewayz.com/hu/blog/the-middle-east-business-platform-report-gcc-startup-infrastructure-and-operational-trends-2026)
- Pemo: SMBs account for 59% of total AI spend in the UAE (Pemo customer base) — [Pemo](https://www.pemo.io/post/saas-spend-management)

### Inferences
- Planning assumption: expect low single-digit free-to-paid conversion (about 2-5%) and 15-22% annual logo churn for a horizontal SMB builder; a 2% card fee on the Free tier monetises the non-converting majority, as Shopify (2% on Basic) and Zbooni (3.5%) do.
- AED 99/month is within what UAE micro-businesses already pay for single tools (Mamo Premium AED 99, Zoho Books AED 60-120, Shopify Basic ≈ AED 118); AED 299 needs a workflow that replaces a staff task or agency retainer (AED 500+/month maintenance, section 7).

### Gaps
- No survey of UAE/GCC SME software budgets or willingness to pay in AED was found; this needs primary research.
- No churn or conversion data specific to GCC builders (Zid, Salla, Zbooni).

## 7. UAE web agency and freelancer prices (the "do nothing new" alternative)

### Takeaway
A basic small-business website in the UAE is commonly quoted at about AED 3,000-15,000 from a freelancer and AED 5,000-50,000+ from an agency, with e-commerce builds at AED 10,000-80,000 and maintenance at AED 500-5,000/month. All figures come from agency marketing blogs and are indicative only.

### Cited Findings
- Dubai web development overall range AED 3,000 to AED 200,000+ — [Cubix 2026 guide](https://www.cubix.co/ae/blog/web-development-cost-in-dubai/)
- Small-business site estimates: AED 4,000-12,000; AED 8,000-15,000 for custom design; AED 7,000-55,000 with CMS and integrations (different agency blogs) — [Red Spider](https://www.redspider.ae/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-develop-a-website-for-small-businesses-in-dubai); [Carril Agency 2026](https://www.carrilagency.com/en/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost-in-dubai); [Element8](https://www.element8.ae/blogs/website-development-cost-dubai)
- Freelancer AED 3,000-15,000 vs agency AED 10,000-100,000; another source says agencies start from AED 5,000 and exceed AED 50,000 for complex work — [RedBerries 2026 pricing guide](https://redberries.ae/web-design-dubai-price/); [Digital Graphiks](https://digitalgraphiks.ae/blog/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-a-website-in-uae)
- Shopify/WooCommerce store builds AED 15,000-60,000 (another: AED 10,000 to 80,000+) — [RedBerries e-commerce cost](https://redberries.ae/how-much-does-it-cost-to-build-an-ecommerce-website-in-the-united-arab-emirates/); [Digital Gravity Shopify cost Dubai](https://www.digitalgravity.ae/blog/how-much-does-shopify-development-cost-in-dubai/)
- Ongoing maintenance AED 500-5,000/month — [Royex](https://www.royex.ae/blog/how-much-does-a-website-cost-in-dubai)

### Inferences
- One year of Renoxium's top tier (AED 299 × 12 = AED 3,588) costs about the same as the cheapest freelancer site and less than a single month of upper-range agency maintenance; the comparison is strongest for custom workflow apps (bookings, order tracking, admin panels), which agencies price well above brochure sites.
- Agency-built sites remain the default for businesses wanting someone else to do it; Renoxium's competitor here is the owner's time and confidence, not price.

### Gaps
- No independent (non-agency) price survey; no data on what share of UAE SMEs use an agency/freelancer vs DIY builders vs nothing.
- Prices for a "simple custom web app" (as opposed to a site) from UAE freelancers were not found separately.
