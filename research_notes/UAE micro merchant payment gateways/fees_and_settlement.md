# UAE micro-merchant payment gateways: published fees and settlement terms (as of November 2026)

All pages accessed 22 November 2026. Amounts in AED unless a provider only publishes USD. "Primary" = provider's own page; "secondary" = third-party listing. Several official pages blocked automated access (Mamo pricing/help: HTTP 403; Paymob UAE pricing: 403; Tap UAE pricing: 404; PayTabs support: login redirect; Network FAQ: 403) — for those, figures come from search-engine snippets of the official page and/or secondary sources and are labelled as such.

Reading aid — confidence labels used below:
- [P] read directly on provider page
- [P-snippet] official page text seen only via search snippet (page itself blocked)
- [S] secondary source only
- [UNPUBLISHED] provider does not publish; contact sales

---

## 1. Transaction fees (local cards, international cards, Amex, wallets, fixed fee)

### Takeaway
Only Ziina, Mamo, Stripe, Telr, Nomod, Foloosi, Zbooni, botim money (headline rate) and PayPal publish UAE rates; the self-serve cluster sits at roughly 2.6-2.9% + AED 0.80-1.00 for local cards with a +0.5% to +1.5% international surcharge. Network International, Magnati, Checkout.com, Tap, PayTabs, Paymob, Amazon Payment Services and Payoneer Checkout either publish nothing in AED or quote per merchant.

### Cited Findings

**Ziina (Business)**
- 2.6% + AED 1 per transaction when receiving via Ziina Links, QR codes, NFC, Tap to Pay, website or Ziina Balance; +1.5% for international cards or non-AED currency. Page updated 29 June 2026. [P] — [Ziina business fees](https://ziina.com/business/help/ae/en/business-fees)
- Tap to Pay on iPhone: same 2.6% + AED 1, +1.5% international; "No hidden fees, no setup costs, no monthly charges". Updated 7 June 2026. [P] — [Ziina Tap to Pay fees](https://ziina.com/help-center/11096189-what-are-the-transaction-fees-for-tap-to-pay)
- An older Ziina help article still lists 2.9% + AED 1 for any card / Apple Pay / Google Pay (discrepancy; 2.6% is the newer figure). [P-snippet] — [Ziina older fee article](https://ziina.com/help-center/6847430-what-are-the-fees-to-use-ziina-business)
- Personal accounts: receiving is free to the recipient; the payer is charged 2.9% + AED 1 when paying a personal account by card/Apple Pay/Google Pay; non-Ziina users paying a Business account by card pay nothing (the business bears the fee). [P] — [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees)

### Inferences
- Kredit.ae figures for Stripe (3.045% + 1.05), Paymob (3.045% + 1.05), Tap (2.8875% + 1.05), Mamo (3.045% + 1.05) and Telr are exactly the ex-VAT rates multiplied by 1.05, so Kredit quotes VAT-inclusive rates; Tap's underlying ex-VAT rate is therefore most likely 2.75% + AED 1.
- For in-person micro-sales, Tap-to-Pay rates with no fixed fee (Nomod 1.67-1.87% standard cards; Foloosi 1.5% + AED 0.10) are materially cheaper than payment links at AED 50-100 tickets, where the AED 0.80-1.00 fixed fee adds 0.8-2.0 percentage points.
- Telr's Startup tier, as published, behaves like a flat AED 249/month subscription for under AED 10,000 volume; crossing AED 10,000 moves the merchant to a costlier tier (see worked example).

### Gaps
- Mamo's live pricing page could not be opened; the non-AED surcharge (1.4% vs 1.5%/2%) and Amex treatment are unresolved.
- No verifiable current AED rate card for Paymob UAE, Tap, PayTabs, APS (image-only), Network, Magnati, Checkout.com, PayBy payment links/SoftPOS detail, or Payoneer Checkout in AED.
- Amex-specific rates are published only by Nomod; Apple Pay/Google Pay are nowhere priced separately (treated as card rate where mentioned).
- No published merchant fee for Aani from any acquirer was found.

## 2. Setup, monthly/annual fees, minimum commitment, deposits/reserves

### Takeaway
Self-serve fintechs (Ziina, Mamo Growth, Stripe, Nomod, Zbooni All-in-One, botim) have no setup or monthly fee; Telr, Mamo Premium, APS and PayTabs Advanced carry monthly fees of roughly AED 99-249, and Network International applies a fall-below fee under AED 20,000/month. No provider publishes a fixed deposit or rolling-reserve percentage.

### Cited Findings
- Ziina: no setup costs, no monthly charges, no subscription or sign-up fees. — [Ziina Tap to Pay fees](https://ziina.com/help-center/11096189-what-are-the-transaction-fees-for-tap-to-pay); [Ziina website fees](https://ziina.com/help-center/12781588-what-are-the-fees-associated-with-using-ziina-on-my-website)
- Mamo: Growth AED 0/month; Premium AED 99/month (cut from AED 149 in Nov 2024); the subscription is not charged in any month with no payments received. [P-snippet] — [Mamo Nov 2024 pricing changes](https://help.mamopay.com/en/articles/10071872-november-2024-pricing-changes); [Mamo pricing plans](https://help.mamopay.com/en/articles/7234173-pricing-plans-and-subscriptions)
- Stripe: no setup or monthly fee on standard pricing. — [Stripe AE pricing](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing)
- Telr: monthly fee AED 249 (Startup) / 199 (Entry) / 99 (Small) / 0 (Medium), all + 5% VAT; setup fee and security deposit not stated. — [Telr pricing](https://telr.com/pricing/)
- Nomod: no monthly subscription listed; extra team members AED 7/month beyond the free allowance (7/5/2 by plan); optional Terminal Duo hardware AED 1,299-1,499 one-time. — [Nomod standard plans](https://nomod.com/docs/standard-plans)
- Foloosi: one-time setup fee AED 150; "no annual maintenance fees or hidden charges". — [Foloosi pricing](https://www.foloosi.com/pricing)
- Zbooni All in One: no setup and no monthly fees. — [Zbooni pricing](https://www.zbooni.com/pricing)
- botim money: no setup fees. — [botim money pricing](https://www.botim.money/pricing)
- APS: no setup or integration fee on Standard [P]; AED 200/month account management [S]. — [APS pricing](https://paymentservices.amazon.com/pricing); [Kredit APS](https://kredit.ae/business/amazon-payment-services). Conflicting third-party setup-fee claims exist (AED 200; AED 1,500 standard / AED 750 for Dubai Chamber members). [S] — [Dubai Chambers APS offer](https://www.dubaichambers.com/en/w/amazon-payment-services)
- PayTabs: USD 50 monthly fee/minimum if volume is under AED 20k on the Advanced plan; Flexi has none (2023 article). Other third-party pages quote AED 183.65/month and AED 918.24 setup (unverified). — [PayTabs pricing article](https://support.paytabs.com/en/support/solutions/articles/60000848540-pricing-plan-starting-of-22-feb-2023-); [Kredit PayTabs](https://kredit.ae/business/paytabs)
- Network International: a fall-below / volume-shortfall fee may apply when monthly sales are AED 20,000 or less; amount is set in the application form. Payout timing and "any reserves or deductions" follow the individual agreement. [S] — [Kredit Network International](https://kredit.ae/business/network-international)
- Payoneer: annual account fee USD 29.95 only if the account receives less than USD 6,000 in 12 consecutive months. — [Payoneer pricing](https://www.payoneer.com/about/pricing/)
- Paymob: no monthly subscription on Standard online plan. [S] — [Kredit Paymob](https://kredit.ae/business/paymob)

### Inferences
- A merchant at AED 5,000-30,000/month is below Network's AED 20,000 threshold for most of that range and below PayTabs' AED 20k threshold, so minimum/fall-below fees are likely to bite with bank-style acquirers.
- A USD 6,000/year receipt threshold (about AED 22,000) means Payoneer's annual fee would not apply to this merchant profile if all volume flowed through it.

### Gaps
- No provider publishes a security-deposit or rolling-reserve figure; all describe reserves (if any) as agreement- or risk-based. Telr and PayTabs deposits historically reported by third parties could not be verified.
- Magnati, Checkout.com, Tap: setup/monthly/minimum fees unpublished.

## 3. Payout/settlement fee, schedule, minimum payout, instant payout

### Takeaway
Published settlement speeds range from next-day (botim, Nomod Next Day plan) to five business days (Stripe, Mamo standard, Tap, Nomod Five Days), and faster settlement is explicitly priced as a rate uplift (Mamo +0.5-0.75%, Nomod +0.3%, Stripe instant 1.5%). Ziina's payout fee is reported inconsistently across its own pages.

### Cited Findings
- Ziina cash-out fee — conflicting official statements: help article updated 24 Sep 2026 says "Ziina doesn't charge a fee for cash-outs" (bank may charge) — [Ziina cash-out article](https://ziina.com/help-center/6847423-do-i-pay-a-fee-to-cash-out); a search snippet of Ziina's help centre says AED 1 per cash-out from 8 January 2026 — [Ziina help centre](https://ziina.com/help-center/12781259-what-are-the-fees-to-use-ziina-business); a secondary source says payouts cost AED 1 each from 1 Nov 2026 — [Startupik](https://startupik.com/cheapest-uae-payment-gateway-for-startups/); the general fees page shows cashouts "Free", including under pricing from 1 Nov 2026 (appears to be the personal schedule) — [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees)
- Ziina payout timing: about 12 hours without a Ziina card; instant with the card, subject to review. [S] — [Kredit gateways](https://kredit.ae/business/payment-gateways)
- Mamo: standard settlement within 5 business days; same-day settlement at +0.75% (Growth) or +0.5% (Premium) per transaction. [P-snippet] — [Mamo pricing](https://www.mamopay.com/pricing). Business terms (updated 25 Sep 2026) describe a daily settlement cycle excluding UAE bank closure days. [P-snippet] — [Mamo business terms](https://www.mamopay.com/legal/terms-business)
- Stripe UAE: initial settlement timing 5 business days; first payout typically 7-14 days after first live payment; minimum payout AED 2; standard payouts free; Instant Payouts 1.5% (min AED 2); USD payouts 1% (min USD 25). — [Stripe payouts docs](https://docs.stripe.com/payouts); [Stripe AE pricing](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing)
- Nomod: payout next day / after two days / after five days depending on plan (the faster plan costs +0.3% on links vs Five Days); payouts Monday-Saturday excluding UAE public holidays. — [Nomod standard plans](https://nomod.com/docs/standard-plans)
- botim money: "One-day payment settlement", automated settlement. — [botim money pricing](https://www.botim.money/pricing)
- Zbooni: "Payouts on demand"; timing not stated. — [Zbooni pricing](https://www.zbooni.com/pricing)
- Tap: AED wallet T+5 with daily payouts; USD wallet T+5 paid weekly on Tuesdays. [S] — [Kredit Tap](https://kredit.ae/business/tap-payments). Other third-party claims of T+1 and T+3 conflict. [S] — [Startupik](https://startupik.com/cheapest-uae-payment-gateway-for-startups/)
- APS: standard UAE settlement weekly; agreement sets actual timing. [S] — [Kredit APS](https://kredit.ae/business/amazon-payment-services)
- Paymob: standard online plan pays out weekly; POS page advertises daily settlement. [S] — [Kredit Paymob](https://kredit.ae/business/paymob)
- Network International: timing and thresholds set in the merchant application. [S] — [Kredit Network International](https://kredit.ae/business/network-international)
- PayPal: withdrawal to a UAE bank account is free when no conversion is involved and the amount exceeds AED 750; AED 11 is shown for the other case (layout ambiguous); withdrawal to card AED 20. — [PayPal AE merchant fees](https://www.paypal.com/ae/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees)
- Payoneer: withdrawal to bank in local currency with no conversion 1.2%-4% (min up to USD 20 in some countries); balance-to-balance conversion 0.5%. No UAE-specific figure on the public page. — [Payoneer pricing](https://www.payoneer.com/about/pricing/)
- Aani: funds transfer and settle "in less than 10 seconds", 24/7 (platform-level claim). — [AEP small merchants FAQ](https://aep.ae/en/frequently-asked-questions/small-merchants-and-sole-proprietors/)
- Telr, Foloosi: settlement timing and payout fees not stated on pricing pages. — [Telr pricing](https://telr.com/pricing/); [Foloosi pricing](https://www.foloosi.com/pricing)
- Other reference points (unverified secondary): NEOPAY online T+3 / POS T+1; e& UTap within two business days; Paymennt 5 working days (Basic/Standard). [S] — [Kredit gateways](https://kredit.ae/business/payment-gateways)

### Inferences
- For a merchant who needs cash quickly, the true comparison is the "fast" price: Mamo Growth same-day is effectively 3.65% + AED 1; Nomod Next Day links 2.67% + AED 0.80; botim's one-day settlement is included in its headline rate; Ziina's roughly 12-hour payout appears included (subject to the unresolved AED 1 cash-out fee).
- If Ziina's AED 1 per cash-out applies, daily cash-outs would add up to about AED 30/month; weekly cash-outs about AED 4-5.

### Gaps
- Ziina's current business cash-out fee (free vs AED 1) and payout timing are not confirmed on a single authoritative page.
- Minimum payout amounts are published only by Stripe (AED 2) and implicitly PayPal (AED 750 threshold).
- Telr, Foloosi, Zbooni, PayTabs, Magnati, Checkout.com settlement schedules unverified.

## 4. Refund fees, chargeback fees, FX mark-up

### Takeaway
Chargeback fees are published only by Stripe (AED 60) and Nomod (AED 277 managed dispute); most others are silent or contractual. Refunds are generally free to issue but the original processing fee is not returned.

### Cited Findings
- Stripe: AED 60 per dispute received; a further AED 60 if countered manually (returned if won); AED 60 for appeals (not returned); Smart Disputes 30% of amount on won disputes. Refunds: no fee, but original processing and conversion fees are not returned. FX: +1% conversion. — [Stripe AE pricing](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing)
- Nomod: managed disputes AED 277 per dispute on all plans; non-AED currencies priced at 3.97%-4.17% + AED 1. — [Nomod standard plans](https://nomod.com/docs/standard-plans)
- PayPal: no fee to refund but original fees not returned; currency conversion 3.0% above base rate on balance conversions/withdrawals/payments received and 4.0% in other cases; chargeback fee has no AED amount ("all other markets" schedule lists USD 10.00). — [PayPal AE merchant fees](https://www.paypal.com/ae/webapps/mpp/merchant-fees)
- Ziina: +1.5% for non-AED currency or international cards; fees page warns the applied exchange rate "may not be the same as the market rate"; refund and chargeback fees not stated. — [Ziina business fees](https://ziina.com/business/help/ae/en/business-fees); [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees)
- Mamo: no fixed chargeback fee found; non-AED surcharge +1.4% to +2% depending on source/plan. — [Mamo pricing](https://www.mamopay.com/pricing); [Startupik](https://startupik.com/cheapest-uae-payment-gateway-for-startups/)
- Tap: no chargeback/refund amounts published; merchant terms assign chargebacks, fines and refund liabilities to the merchant; FX component 2-5% on non-USD foreign currency. [S] — [Kredit Tap](https://kredit.ae/business/tap-payments). A third-party test account reported USD 25 per dispute. [S] — [Startupik](https://startupik.com/cheapest-uae-payment-gateway-for-startups/)
- Paymob: chargeback AED 75 according to a third-party site, not on Paymob's site. [S] — [Startupik](https://startupik.com/cheapest-uae-payment-gateway-for-startups/)
- APS: processing fees remain on a refund; "no fixed UAE chargeback price is published". [S] — [Kredit APS](https://kredit.ae/business/amazon-payment-services)
- Network International: processing fees are not returned on refunds; chargebacks can be deducted from settlement; no fee amount. [S] — [Kredit Network International](https://kredit.ae/business/network-international)
- Payoneer: conversion 0.5% between balances; 1.2%-4% on withdrawals involving conversion. — [Payoneer pricing](https://www.payoneer.com/about/pricing/)
- Telr, Foloosi, Zbooni, botim: refund/chargeback fees not stated on pricing pages. — [Telr pricing](https://telr.com/pricing/); [Foloosi pricing](https://www.foloosi.com/pricing); [Zbooni pricing](https://www.zbooni.com/pricing); [botim money pricing](https://www.botim.money/pricing)

### Inferences
- With AED 50-300 tickets, a single chargeback fee (AED 60-277) can exceed the sale value several times over; the published spread between Stripe and Nomod is large enough to matter for higher-dispute categories.
- PayPal's 3-4% FX spread on top of 3.4-4.4% makes it the most expensive route for AED-denominated domestic sales.

### Gaps
- Chargeback fees for Ziina, Mamo, Telr, Foloosi, Zbooni, botim, PayTabs, Magnati, Network, Checkout.com are not published or not found.
- Whether fixed AED fees are returned on refund is not stated by most providers.

## 5. VAT treatment of fees

### Takeaway
5% UAE VAT applies to the provider's fee (not the sale amount) essentially everywhere; most published rates are VAT-exclusive, with Ziina's wording the notable ambiguity.

### Cited Findings
- Telr: "All fees and charges shown above are subject to 5% VAT." — [Telr pricing](https://telr.com/pricing/)
- Mamo: all prices exclusive of VAT; 5% VAT charged only on the transaction fee; Mamo's own example for an AED 100 UAE card payment on Growth is AED 3.9 fee, AED 4.1 after VAT. [P-snippet] — [Mamo pricing](https://www.mamopay.com/pricing); [Mamo pricing plans](https://help.mamopay.com/en/articles/7234173-pricing-plans-and-subscriptions)
- Nomod: fixed 5% VAT on all fees. — [Nomod standard plans](https://nomod.com/docs/standard-plans)
- Zbooni: 3.5% + AED 1 per order "+ VAT". — [Zbooni pricing](https://www.zbooni.com/pricing)
- Ziina: "All fees include 5% VAT (applied to the fee only, not your transaction amount)" on the business fee article; the general fees page says all fees "carry" a standard 5% VAT applied to the fee. — [Ziina business fees](https://ziina.com/business/help/ae/en/business-fees); [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees)
- Stripe: no VAT rate shown; says tax is charged on certain products based on location and tax status. — [Stripe AE pricing](https://stripe.com/ae/pricing)
- Network International FAQ example quoted as "AED 229 + VAT". [P-snippet] — [Network FAQ](https://www.network.ae/faqs/unified-br-group-email-br-domain)
- A secondary comparison states all its listed fees exclude 5% VAT and that VAT applies to every provider's fees. [S] — [Startupik](https://startupik.com/cheapest-uae-payment-gateway-for-startups/)
- Foloosi, botim, PayPal, Payoneer: VAT treatment not stated on pricing pages. — [Foloosi pricing](https://www.foloosi.com/pricing); [botim money pricing](https://www.botim.money/pricing)

### Inferences
- If Ziina's 2.6% + AED 1 is genuinely VAT-inclusive, its ex-VAT equivalent is about 2.48% + AED 0.95, widening its advantage over Mamo/Stripe (2.9% + AED 1 ex-VAT = 3.045% + AED 1.05 inc-VAT). The worked example therefore shows Ziina as a range.
- A non-VAT-registered micro-merchant (below the AED 375,000 mandatory threshold) cannot reclaim the 5%, so VAT-inclusive cost is the relevant number for this segment. (Threshold is general UAE VAT knowledge, not sourced here.)

### Gaps
- Ziina's VAT-inclusive vs VAT-on-top position needs confirmation from an actual statement/invoice.
- Stripe UAE's practical VAT charging to UAE-resident accounts not confirmed on its pricing page.

## 6. Free tiers, promotions, personal vs business accounts

### Takeaway
No provider offers a genuinely free card-acceptance tier; "free" means no subscription. Ziina is the only provider with a clear personal-account route (payer bears 2.9% + AED 1), and Telr's Startup tier is a flat-subscription structure rather than a free tier.

### Cited Findings
- Ziina personal: receiving is free for the recipient; a payer using a card/Apple Pay/Google Pay pays 2.9% + AED 1; Ziina-balance-to-balance is free. Ziina Violet membership AED 100/month or AED 996/year incl. VAT (consumer plan). — [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees)
- Ziina business: the business bears 2.6% + AED 1; payers are not charged. — [Ziina fees](https://ziina.com/fees); [Ziina business fees](https://ziina.com/business/help/ae/en/business-fees)
- Ziina help centre title references a "New fee from 8 November 2025" for personal accounts (content not read). — [Ziina personal fees article](https://ziina.com/help-center/12259572-what-are-the-fees-for-a-personal-account)
- Mamo Growth: AED 0 subscription; Premium not billed in months with no payments. — [Mamo pricing plans](https://help.mamopay.com/en/articles/7234173-pricing-plans-and-subscriptions)
- Telr Startup: AED 249/month with 0% + AED 0 transaction fee under AED 10,000 volume. — [Telr pricing](https://telr.com/pricing/)
- Zbooni All in One: free plan, 3.5% + AED 1 + VAT. — [Zbooni pricing](https://www.zbooni.com/pricing)
- APS via Dubai Chambers: setup fee discounted AED 1,500 to AED 750 and the AED 1 per-transaction fee waived for the first three months (member offer; date not verified). [S] — [Dubai Chambers APS offer](https://www.dubaichambers.com/en/w/amazon-payment-services)
- NEOPAY NEO BIZ offer: online 2.15% + AED 0.50, POS 1.97% domestic. [S] — [Kredit gateways](https://kredit.ae/business/payment-gateways)
- Aani: positioned for small merchants and sole proprietors; fees left to the participating bank/PSP. — [AEP small merchants FAQ](https://aep.ae/en/frequently-asked-questions/small-merchants-and-sole-proprietors/)

### Inferences
- A seller without a trade licence can in practice only use personal-account routes (Ziina personal, bank transfer/Aani), where card costs fall on the payer at 2.9% + AED 1; all gateway pricing above assumes a licensed business.
- Bank-transfer/Aani acceptance is plausibly the lowest-cost route (possibly zero), but that is not documented by any published merchant tariff found.

### Gaps
- Eligibility rules (trade-licence requirement) per provider were out of scope of the pages read.
- No current time-limited promotions were found on primary pages.
